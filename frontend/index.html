Three beats, one curve

01

Launch — prove it is yours

Anyone submits a meme with its origin story. GenLayer validators read the evidence independently and must agree before the listing goes live. No agreement, no listing — the listing stays pending and can be retried.

02

Climb — every trade moves the price

A constant-product curve prices every token. Buy and the price climbs; sell and it falls. A 1% fee splits 0.3% to the creator and 0.7% to the platform pool. Nothing here is a guess — the price is the curve.

03

Graduate — 85 GEN of net buys

When cumulative net buys reach 85 GEN, anyone can trigger graduation. The curve locks, the fill at that moment is recorded, and the listing graduates. Moon Cat already did — 74.81% filled.
